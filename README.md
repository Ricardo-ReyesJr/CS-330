- What new design skill has your work on the project helped you to craft?
Having to think a little bit like an artist helped me to visualize the design idea.
- What design process did you follow in your project work?
imagining everything as primitive shapes.
- How could tactics from your design approach be applied in future work?
Simplifying things helps when it comes to design and art. This is how all 3D art is, even the human body starts with primitive shapes. circles, squares, and cylinders.
